PART 1 — THE PROBLEM  (0:00 – 0:40)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Indian Railways runs the world's largest rail network under one
management — over 13,000 trains a day, 68,000 km of track.

Keeping that infrastructure safe means maintenance teams must work
inside "Engineering Blocks" — short windows, usually 2–4 hours at
night, when a section of track is cleared of trains.

Today, that scheduling is done manually.
  - A maintenance manager looks at a spreadsheet.
  - They assign tasks one by one, based on experience.
  - They have no real-time visibility into which tasks are most
    overdue, which windows are about to expire, or which tasks
    from two different departments could be done simultaneously
    in the same block.

The result?
  - High-debt tasks slip through the cracks and become safety risks.
  - Blocks get used at 40–50% efficiency when they could be at 80%+.
  - Every extra possession taken means one more disruption to
    passenger train movements.

The question is: can we bring intelligent, explainable automation
to this process — without replacing the human decision-maker?

PART 2 — OUR SOLUTION  (0:40 – 1:45)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

We built RAILFUSE — Opportunity-Aware Adaptive Block Planning.

The core idea is simple:
  Every maintenance block is not just a time window —
  it is an OPPORTUNITY to fuse multiple tasks from multiple
  departments into one possession.

RAILFUSE does five things, in order, every time it runs:

  1. MAINTENANCE DEBT SCORING
     We score every pending task using a transparent formula that
     factors in how many days it is overdue, how many times it has
     been deferred, and how safety-critical it is. This tells us
     WHAT needs to happen most urgently.

  2. FLEXIBILITY SCORING
     We calculate how many future blocks each task can still fit
     into. A task with only one future option gets a protection
     flag — it will not be bumped by a less urgent task. This
     tells us HOW MUCH TIME we have left to act.

  3. CONFLICT DETECTION
     Before assigning anything, we check: Is there a train in this
     section within a 10-minute safety buffer? Are the required
     resources available? Does the task duration fit the block's
     remaining capacity? All checks run in real time.

  4. MULTI-DEPARTMENT OPPORTUNITY FUSION
     This is our core innovation. Engineering and S&T teams rarely
     coordinate. RAILFUSE builds a compatibility graph — which tasks
     from different departments can share a block safely — and fuses
     compatible tasks. One possession. Two departments. Zero extra
     disruption.

  5. FULL EXPLAINABILITY
     Every task gets a plain-language reason:
     "SELECTED for BLK003: maintenance debt 28.5, fused with T004
      (S&T, compatible), 45 min remaining capacity."
     "DEFERRED: 3 future windows available, BLK002 is a better fit."
     No black box. Every decision can be audited.

On the dashboard you can see this live —
  - 25 maintenance tasks scored and ranked
  - 10 blocks across 4 corridors: Delhi, Agra, Lucknow, Bengaluru
  - 34 train movements tracked, safety buffers enforced
  - Full optimizer output with per-task explanations

And if a critical track defect is discovered at 2 AM?
  - Go to Dynamic Re-planning
  - Fill in the defect details
  - Click Inject & Re-optimize
  - In seconds, the full plan is recalculated around the emergency
  - You see exactly which tasks moved, which block absorbed the
    new task, and why

Traditional systems would require a phone call, a manual update,
and a new spreadsheet. RAILFUSE does it in one click.

PART 3 — HOW WE ARE DIFFERENT  (1:45 – 2:30)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Existing systems fall into two categories:

  CATEGORY A — Manual Scheduling Tools
  (Spreadsheets, basic IRMS modules)
    - No scoring. No prioritization. No conflict detection.
    - Cannot combine tasks across departments.
    - Cannot adapt when a new emergency task arrives.
    - The human does everything. The system just stores data.

  CATEGORY B — Academic OR Commercial Optimizers
  (General MIP solvers, generic scheduling libraries)
    - Not built for Indian Railways' specific constraints.
    - No concept of maintenance debt or flexibility score.
    - No multi-department fusion logic.
    - No explainability — output is a schedule, not a reason.
    - Cannot do real-time replanning from a UI.

RAILFUSE sits in a different space entirely:

  WHAT WE DO DIFFERENTLY           WHY IT MATTERS
  ─────────────────────────────── ─────────────────────────────────
  Maintenance Debt formula          Quantifies urgency — no guessing
  Flexibility Score + Protection    Prevents stranded low-flex tasks
  Multi-Dept Compatibility Graph    Maximizes each possession's value
  Zero-Possession Preference        Reduces disruptions to passengers
  Real-Time Dynamic Replanning      Handles emergencies without calls
  Per-Task Plain-Language Reasons   Builds trust with field officers
  Weekly + Monthly Horizon View     Supports strategic capacity planning
  103 deterministic tests           Verifiable, reproducible behavior

We are not claiming to solve a problem no one has studied.
Block scheduling, constraint satisfaction, and explainability are
established fields. What RAILFUSE does is combine these specifically
for the Indian Railways maintenance context — debt-aware scoring,
cross-department fusion, and dynamic replanning — into a single,
coherent, demonstrable workflow.

That integration is our contribution.

LIKELY JUDGE QUESTIONS — QUICK ANSWERS

Q: Is this connected to real Indian Railways data?
A: No. All data is synthetic, purpose-built for this demonstration.
   The system design is ready to plug into real IRMS data exports.

Q: How is the optimizer different from a simple greedy algorithm?
A: It is a multi-pass heuristic. Pass 1 scores and ranks all tasks.
   Pass 2 checks conflicts. Pass 3 finds compatible combinations.
   Pass 4 applies zero-possession preference. Pass 5 protects
   low-flexibility tasks for future windows. A greedy algorithm
   would just take the highest-debt task for each block without
   considering cross-block impact or future stranding.

Q: Why not use a neural network or ML model?
A: Explainability is a hard requirement for safety-critical
   infrastructure. A neural model cannot tell a field officer
   WHY a task was deferred. Our rule-based heuristic with
   transparent scoring can. We chose correctness and trust
   over prediction accuracy.

Q: What would it take to deploy this at a real division?
A: Three things: (1) A data feed from the existing IRMS/SAP
   system for tasks and blocks. (2) A REST API wrapper around
   that feed. (3) This backend deployed on a divisional server.
   The frontend runs in any browser — no client install needed.

Q: How does this compare to what Railways already uses?
A: Existing tools are primarily record-keeping systems — they
   store what was done. RAILFUSE is a decision-support system —
   it tells you what to do next and why. That is a fundamentally
   different category of tool.

Q: Is the optimizer scalable to thousands of tasks?
A: The current implementation handles the SIH demonstration scale.
   For production scale, the heuristic approach can be parallelized
   by corridor/division, and the conflict detection can be indexed
   for O(log n) lookups. This is a prototype; production engineering
   would address scale.
